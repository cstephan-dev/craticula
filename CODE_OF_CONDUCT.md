# Code of conduct

Craticula is a small project, and we want it to stay a place where people can ask questions, disagree about a design and
report a mistake without being treated badly. This code adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as the standard we hold ourselves to. What follows is the short form.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to the topic.
- Criticize the code and the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are not sure what someone meant.
- Accept review and decisions graciously; a maintainer has the last word on what is merged.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language, in any form.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) and wherever someone clearly speaks for the project.

## How to report a problem

Use a private route, so that no one has to raise it in public:

1. Open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
2. Begin the title with `Conduct:` and describe what happened. Only the maintainers can read it.

*This route is the one we can offer today. If we publish a contact address for conduct reports, it will replace this section.*

## What we do about it

The maintainers read every report, treat it as confidential, and answer the person who reported it. Depending on what
happened, the answer can be a private reminder, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
